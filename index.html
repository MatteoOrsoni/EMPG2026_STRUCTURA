About this poster

Overview

STRUCTURA is a framework designed to support the interpretation of individual test scores within network psychometrics. Whereas most current approaches in network psychometrics primarily treat psychological data at the aggregate or group level, STRUCTURA shifts the focus to the person and to the configuration of single observed responses. In this way, it offers a principled method for examining how individual score profiles can be interpreted within a network-based measurement perspective, thereby contributing to the advancement of measurement theory in network psychometrics. The poster introduces the general rationale of the framework and illustrates its application through signed SI profiles, including extensions to GSI and TESS, in order to show how local and person-specific response patterns can yield substantively meaningful information beyond summary-level analyses.
