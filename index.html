About this poster

Overview

STRUCTURA is a Bayesian Network framework for person-level scoring and behavioral anomaly detection in cognitive assessment. While most approaches in network psychometrics operate at the group level, STRUCTURA shifts the focus to the individual, translating the topology of a learned Bayesian Network into interpretable scores from a single response pattern. The framework introduces three complementary metrics: TESS, a structural ability estimate that adjusts for response inconsistency; GSI, a global measure of how improbable the overall pattern is; and LSI, signed item-level anomaly flags that pinpoint the specific items driving the inconsistency. The poster illustrates the approach through a Guttman paradox case from Raven's CPM normative data, showing how STRUCTURA can reveal clinically meaningful information that summary scores alone cannot capture.
